collection: portfolio
---

Time-series study of the dynamic relationships among inflation, GDP growth, and unemployment in China from 1980 to 2020.

I used VAR and SVAR models, tested stationarity with ADF tests, and implemented the work in Stata. The results point to a short-run influence of inflation and GDP shocks on unemployment.

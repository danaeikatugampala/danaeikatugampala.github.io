Bachelor’s thesis in Economics, University of Ruhuna.

This project asks how green bonds help mobilize climate finance in South Asia and what limits their uptake. The design is qualitative: a systematic literature review plus semi-structured interviews with Sri Lankan financial institutions.

The thesis assesses the contribution of green bonds to climate finance and identifies policy and market barriers that constrain the instrument in the region. It sits at the intersection of environmental economics, sustainable finance, and environmental policy.

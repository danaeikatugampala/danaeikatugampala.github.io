In-depth statistical study of customer satisfaction in Sri Lanka’s hospitality sector using Booking.com data.

The analysis focuses on service quality, staff service, facilities, price, and location. I used correlation, regression, and factor analysis to identify the main predictors of satisfaction. The fitted model had an R² of 95.5%.

Selected projects
======
* **Factors Influencing Customer Satisfaction in the Hospitality Industry in Sri Lanka**
  * Statistical analysis of customer satisfaction using Booking.com data, focusing on service quality, staff service, facilities, price, and location
  * Correlation, regression, and factor analysis; model R² of 95.5%
* **How Interest Rates Affect Economic Growth in Singapore**
  * OLS analysis of 40 years of data (1982–2022) on interest rates, global growth, and Singapore’s GDP
  * Econometric modeling in Gretl, interpreted through monetarist and Keynesian frameworks
* **How Inflation and GDP Growth Affect the Unemployment Rate in China**
  * Time-series analysis using VAR and SVAR on inflation, GDP growth, and unemployment (1980–2020)
  * ADF tests for stationarity and implementation in Stata; short-run influence of inflation and GDP shocks on unemployment

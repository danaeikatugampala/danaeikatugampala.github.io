I am an M.A. student in Economics at [Old Dominion University](https://www.odu.edu/) and a Teaching Assistant and Research Assistant in the Strome Business School. I hold a B.A. (Hons) in Economics from the [University of Ruhuna](https://www.ruh.ac.lk/), Sri Lanka, with a GPA of 3.66/4.00.

I have a strong background in econometrics, economic research, quantitative analysis, and data-driven problem solving, with experience using R, Stata, SPSS, and Gretl. I conduct empirical research, analyze economic data, and communicate research findings. I am interested in applying these skills to consulting projects in healthcare economics, public policy, and industry analysis.

My bachelor’s thesis, *The Role of Green Bonds in Mobilizing Climate Finance in South Asia*, used a qualitative design that combined a systematic literature review with semi-structured interviews at Sri Lankan financial institutions to assess how green bonds contribute to climate finance and to identify policy and market barriers.

## Research interests

- International trade and finance
- Industrial economics
- Applied economics
- Microeconomics and macroeconomics

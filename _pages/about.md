I am an M.A. student in Economics at [Old Dominion University](https://www.odu.edu/)’s Strome College of Business and a Graduate Assistant in the Strome Business School. I recently completed a B.A. (Hons) in Economics at the [University of Ruhuna](https://www.ruh.ac.lk/), Sri Lanka, with a GPA of 3.66/4.00.

My research interests are in **environmental and green economics**, sustainable finance, and applied econometrics. My bachelor’s thesis, *The Role of Green Bonds in Mobilizing Climate Finance in South Asia*, used a qualitative design that combined a systematic literature review with semi-structured interviews at Sri Lankan financial institutions to assess how green bonds contribute to climate finance and to identify policy and market barriers.

I also work with quantitative methods. Independent and course projects have used OLS, VAR/SVAR, correlation, regression, and factor analysis in Gretl and Stata to study interest rates and growth, inflation and unemployment, and customer satisfaction in hospitality.

I am interested in teaching economics and in contributing to research on sustainable finance and environmental policy.

## Research interests

- Environmental economics and green finance
- International trade and finance
- Industrial and applied economics
- Microeconomics and macroeconomics

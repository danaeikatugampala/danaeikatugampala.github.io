Bachelor’s thesis, Department of Economics, University of Ruhuna.

The study uses a qualitative approach that combines a systematic literature review with semi-structured interviews at Sri Lankan financial institutions. It assesses how green bonds contribute to climate finance in South Asia and identifies policy and market barriers to their wider use.

Extended abstract presented at the RASAS 101 Symposium, University of Ruhuna.

The study explores the socio-cultural, psychological, and economic impacts of motherhood on women’s career trajectories in South Asia. It uses secondary data analysis, with attention to thematic patterns and policy implications for gender equity in professional settings.
